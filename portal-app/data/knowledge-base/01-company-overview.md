# Airline Operations Overview

## Company Mission
We are committed to safe, reliable, and efficient air transportation while maintaining the highest standards of customer service and operational excellence.

## Key Departments

### Flight Operations
- **Flight Dispatch**: Coordinates flight planning, weather monitoring, and fuel calculations
- **Crew Scheduling**: Manages pilot and cabin crew rosters ensuring regulatory compliance
- **Flight Training**: Conducts recurrent training and certification programs

### Ground Operations
- **Ramp Services**: Aircraft handling, baggage management, and ground support equipment
- **Station Operations**: Airport check-in, gate management, and passenger services
- **Cargo Operations**: Freight handling, special cargo (live animals, perishables, dangerous goods)

### Maintenance & Engineering
- **Line Maintenance**: Pre-flight and transit inspections
- **Base Maintenance**: Heavy checks (A-check, C-check, D-check) and component overhaul
- **Engineering**: Airworthiness directives, modifications, and reliability monitoring

## Operational Metrics

### Punctuality KPIs
- **On-Time Performance (OTP)**: Flight departs/arrives within 15 minutes of scheduled time
- **Aircraft Utilization**: Average hours flown per aircraft per day (target: 10-12 hours for narrow-body)
- **Turnaround Time**: Time between aircraft arrival and next departure (target: 45-60 minutes for short-haul)

### Safety Metrics
- **Dispatch Reliability**: Percentage of flights departing without technical delays (target: >99.5%)
- **Incident Rate**: Reportable incidents per 100,000 flight hours
- **Maintenance Man-Hours per Flight Hour**: Efficiency indicator

## Standard Operating Procedures

### Irregular Operations (IROPS)
1. **Weather Delays**: Monitor forecasts 24h in advance, pre-position reserve crews
2. **Aircraft Substitution**: Maintain spares at hub airports when possible
3. **Crew Legality**: Track duty time limits per regulatory requirements
4. **Passenger Rebooking**: Priority order: medical, unaccompanied minors, elite status, fare class

### Fuel Management
- Fuel hedging policy: Hedge 30-50% of projected consumption 12-18 months forward
- Flight planning tools: Jeppesen, Lido, or equivalent
- Standard contingency fuel: 5% of planned trip fuel or 5 minutes holding (whichever is greater)

## Seasonal Considerations

### Summer Operations (Peak Season)
- Increased thunderstorm activity in hub regions
- Higher passenger volumes requiring extended check-in times
- Aircraft performance limitations at high-altitude airports in hot weather

### Winter Operations
- De-icing procedures: Type I (de-ice), Type IV (anti-ice) fluid application
- Snow removal priority: Active runways, taxiways, then apron areas
- Cold weather limitations: Minimum oil temperatures, hydraulic fluid viscosity

## Customer Service Standards

### Service Recovery Hierarchy
1. Safety and security issues (immediate priority)
2. Medical emergencies
3. Unaccompanied minors
4. Passengers with disabilities
5. Mileage program elite members
6. Full-fare passengers
7. Discount fare passengers

### Communication Protocols
- **Flight Delays >30 minutes**: Automated notifications via app/SMS
- **Cancellations**: Rebooking options presented within 15 minutes
- **Tarmac Delays**: Update every 30 minutes if aircraft at gate, every 60 minutes if holding

# Aviation Metrics and KPIs

## Revenue Metrics

### Revenue Per Available Seat Mile (RASM)
**Definition**: Total revenue divided by available seat miles (ASM)
**Formula**: `RASM = Total Revenue / (Number of Seats × Distance Flown)`
**Benchmark**: Industry average varies by region; legacy carriers typically 12-18 cents, LCCs 8-12 cents
**Calculation**: Sum all passenger, cargo, and ancillary revenue, divide by total ASMs for the period

### Cost Per Available Seat Mile (CASM)
**Definition**: Total operating cost per unit of capacity
**Formula**: `CASM = Total Operating Costs / ASM`
**Benchmark**: Legacy carriers 13-17 cents, LCCs 7-11 cents
**Ex-Fuel CASM**: CASM excluding fuel costs, better indicator of operational efficiency

### Passenger Revenue Per Available Seat Mile (PRASM)
**Definition**: Passenger revenue component of RASM
**Formula**: `PRASM = Passenger Revenue / ASM`

## Traffic Metrics

### Available Seat Miles (ASM)
**Definition**: Measure of airline capacity
**Formula**: `ASM = Number of Available Seats × Miles Flown`
**Usage**: Indicates total capacity offered to the market

### Revenue Passenger Miles (RPM)
**Definition**: Measure of traffic volume
**Formula**: `RPM = Number of Paying Passengers × Miles Flown`
**Also Known As**: Revenue Passenger Kilometers (RPK) in metric regions

### Load Factor
**Definition**: Percentage of available seats filled with paying passengers
**Formula**: `Load Factor = RPM / ASM × 100%`
**Benchmark**: Industry healthy range 80-85%; above 85% may indicate under-capacity
**Break-even Load Factor**: Load factor at which revenue equals operating costs

### Passenger Yield
**Definition**: Average fare per mile flown per passenger
**Formula**: `Yield = Passenger Revenue / RPM`
**Trend**: Declining due to LCC competition; measured in cents per RPM

## Operational Metrics

### Aircraft Utilization
**Definition**: Average hours aircraft is airborne per day
**Formula**: `Utilization = Total Flight Hours / Number of Aircraft / Days in Period`
**Benchmark**: Narrow-body: 10-12 hours/day, Wide-body: 12-16 hours/day

### Stage Length
**Definition**: Average distance per flight leg
**Formula**: `Stage Length = Total Miles Flown / Number of Flights`
**Impact**: Longer stage lengths typically yield better unit economics

### Turnaround Time
**Definition**: Ground time between arrival and departure
**Breakdown**:
- Deplaning: 5-10 minutes
- Cleaning: 10-15 minutes
- Catering: 10-15 minutes
- Fueling: 15-25 minutes
- Boarding: 20-30 minutes
- **Total Target**: 45-60 minutes for narrow-body, 60-90 for wide-body

## Financial Metrics

### EBITDAR
**Definition**: Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, Amortization, and Rent
**Usage**: Common airline profitability metric, especially for leased fleets

### Operating Margin
**Definition**: Operating profit as percentage of revenue
**Formula**: `Operating Margin = (Operating Revenue - Operating Costs) / Operating Revenue × 100%`
**Benchmark**: 8-15% for healthy airlines; varies significantly by market

### CASK (Cost per Available Seat Kilometer)
**Metric Standard**: European/Asian standard (vs CASM in US)
**Formula**: `CASK = Operating Costs / (Seats × Kilometers Flown)`

## Ancillary Revenue

### Ancillary Revenue Per Passenger
**Definition**: Non-ticket revenue per passenger
**Components**: Baggage fees, seat selection, priority boarding, food/beverage, WiFi
**Benchmark**: ULCCs achieve $40-60, legacy carriers $20-40 per passenger

### Ancillary Penetration Rate
**Definition**: Percentage of passengers purchasing ancillary products
**Example**: If 30% of passengers pay for checked bags, bag fee penetration is 30%

## Quality and Service Metrics

### On-Time Performance (OTP)
**Definition**: Percentage of flights arriving within 15 minutes of schedule
**DOT Definition (US)**: Flight is on-time if arrives less than 15 minutes after scheduled
**ACI Definition (Europe)**: Within 15 minutes of schedule
**Industry Target**: >80%

### Mishandled Baggage Rate
**Definition**: Bags delayed, damaged, or lost per 1,000 passengers
**Formula**: `(Mishandled Bags / Total Passengers) × 1000`
**Industry Average**: 3-6 bags per 1,000 passengers

### Denied Boarding Rate
**Definition**: Passengers denied boarding against their will per 10,000 passengers
**Regulatory Limits**: US DOT requires compensation over 2 per 10,000

### Customer Complaint Rate
**Definition**: Complaints filed per 100,000 passengers
**Categories**: Flight problems, baggage, reservations, fares, customer service

## Fleet Metrics

### Average Fleet Age
**Calculation**: Sum of (Aircraft Age × Number of Aircraft) / Total Fleet Size
**Industry Trend**: Moving toward younger fleets (8-12 years average) for fuel efficiency

### Fleet Utilization by Type
**Narrow-body (A320/B737)**: 10-12 hours/day
**Wide-body (A330/B777)**: 12-16 hours/day
**Regional (E175/CRJ)**: 8-10 hours/day

### Seat Density
**Definition**: Seats per aircraft type
**Trend**: Increasing "densification" - LCCs operate A320s at 180 seats vs 150 for full-service

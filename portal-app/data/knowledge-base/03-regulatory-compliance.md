# Regulatory Compliance and Safety

## Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) - US Operations

### Part 121 Certification
**Applicability**: Scheduled operations with aircraft >9 seats or >7,500 lbs MTOW
**Key Requirements**:
- Airworthiness Certificate
- Operations Specifications (OpSpecs)
- Approved Maintenance Program
- Flight Operations Manual
- Training Programs (approved by FAA)

### Flight Time Limitations (14 CFR Part 117)
**Flight Duty Period (FDP) Limits**:
- Report time 0000-0359: Max 9 hours FDP
- Report time 0400-0459: Max 10 hours FDP
- Report time 0500-0659: Max 11 hours FDP
- Report time 0700-1259: Max 13 hours FDP
- Report time 1300-2159: Max 12 hours FDP
- Report time 2200-2259: Max 11 hours FDP
- Report time 2300-2359: Max 10 hours FDP

**Flight Time Limits**:
- 8 hours between required rest periods (can be extended by splits)
- 30 hours flight time maximum in any 7 consecutive days
- 100 hours maximum in any 28 consecutive days
- 1,000 hours maximum in any calendar year

**Rest Requirements**:
- Minimum 10 hours rest before FDP
- Must provide opportunity for 8 hours sleep
- Rest facility classifications: Class 1 (bunk), Class 2 (seat), Class 3 (cabin seat)

### Maintenance Requirements (14 CFR Part 43 & 145)
**A, B, C, D Check System**:
- **A Check**: Every 500-800 flight hours, 8-12 hours duration
- **B Check**: Every 4-6 months, 1-3 days
- **C Check**: Every 20-24 months, 1-2 weeks
- **D Check**: Every 6-10 years, 4-8 weeks (heavy maintenance visit)

**Airworthiness Directives (ADs)**:
- Emergency ADs: Compliance before further flight
- Standard ADs: Typically 30-90 days compliance window
- Bi-weekly publication in Federal Register

### Required Inspection Items (14 CFR Part 121.701)
- Annual inspection
- 100-hour inspection (if operated commercially)
- Continuous Airworthiness Maintenance Program (CAMP)
- Aging Aircraft Inspections (for aircraft >14 years)

## International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO)

### Annex 6 Standards
**Part I - International Commercial Air Transport (Aeroplanes)**:
- Flight time, flight duty periods, and rest periods
- Fatigue management requirements
- Operations manual content
- Dangerous goods transport
- Security procedures

### Annex 13 - Aircraft Accident Investigation
**Investigation Authority**: State of occurrence has primary responsibility
**Accident Definition**: Occurrence where:
- Person is fatally or seriously injured
- Aircraft sustains substantial damage
- Aircraft is missing or completely inaccessible

**Incident Definition**: Occurrence (other than accident) affecting safety of operation

## European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA)

### Part-CAT (Commercial Air Transport)
**Operations Manual Structure** (AMC1 ORO.MLR.100):
1. General policies and procedures
2. Operational control
3. Flight operations
4. Dangerous goods
5. Security
6. Safety management

### Flight Time Limitations (EU Reg 965/2012)
**Maximum Daily Flight Duty**:
- Unacclimatized crew: 11 hours
- Acclimatized crew: 12 hours (13 with in-flight rest)
- Extended FDP: 14 hours (with augmented crew)

**Cumulative Limits**:
- 100 hours flight time in any 28 consecutive days
- 900 hours flight time in any calendar year
- 1,000 hours flight time in any 12 consecutive months

### Safety Management System (SMS) (EU Reg 965/2012 Annex III)
**Four Components**:
1. Safety policy and objectives
2. Safety risk management (hazard identification, risk assessment)
3. Safety assurance (monitoring, measurement, continuous improvement)
4. Safety promotion (training, communication)

## Safety Management

### IATA Operational Safety Audit (IOSA)
**Renewal Cycle**: Every 2 years
**Standards**: Over 900 audit areas covering:
- Corporate organization
- Safety management
- Operational control
- Flight operations
- Cabin operations
- Ground handling operations
- Aircraft engineering and maintenance
- Cargo operations
- Security

### Safety Performance Indicators (SPIs)
**Leading Indicators** (predictive):
- Crew training completion rates
- Safety report submission rates
- Maintenance task completion on-time
- Standard operating procedure adherence rates

**Lagging Indicators** (outcome-based):
- Accident rate per million departures
- Serious incident rate
- Hull loss rate
- Fatal accident rate

### Flight Data Monitoring (FDM) / Flight Operations Quality Assurance (FOQA)
**Parameters Monitored**:
- Excessive bank angles (>30 degrees)
- Low energy on approach (<Vref)
- Hard landings (>2.0g vertical)
- Unstable approaches
- Long landings (> touchdown zone)
- GPWS/EGPWS activations

**Event Categories**:
- Level 1 (Low risk): Trend monitoring
- Level 2 (Medium risk): Crew debrief
- Level 3 (High risk): Investigation required

## Dangerous Goods

### IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR)
**Classes of Dangerous Goods**:
- Class 1: Explosives
- Class 2: Gases (flammable, non-flammable, toxic)
- Class 3: Flammable liquids
- Class 4: Flammable solids
- Class 5: Oxidizing substances
- Class 6: Toxic and infectious substances
- Class 7: Radioactive materials
- Class 8: Corrosives
- Class 9: Miscellaneous dangerous goods

**Pilot Notification Requirements**:
- NOTOC (Notification to Captain) required for all DG shipments
- Must be delivered before departure
- Contains: Location, class, quantity, emergency response code

## Security Regulations

### TSA Regulations (US)
**Access Control**:
- Security Identification Display Area (SIDA) badges
- Criminal history records check (CHRC) required
- Recurrent training every 2 years

**Known Shipper Program**:
- Only known shippers can tender cargo on passenger aircraft
- Validation required before acceptance

### ICAO Annex 17 - Security
**Security Controls**:
- Passenger and cabin baggage screening
- Hold baggage screening
- Cargo and mail screening
- Access control to security restricted areas
- Aircraft security checks

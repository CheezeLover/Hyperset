# Airline Terminology and Definitions

## Fleet and Aircraft Terms

### Aircraft Types by Size
**Regional Jets**:
- Embraer E175: 76 seats, typical range 2,200 nm
- Bombardier CRJ900: 76-90 seats
- **Definition**: Aircraft with <100 seats, used for feeder routes

**Narrow-Body (Single-Aisle)**:
- Boeing 737 family: 126-230 seats (MAX 7/8/9/10)
- Airbus A320 family: 140-244 seats (neo variants)
- **Definition**: Single aisle, 3-6 hour range, workhorse of domestic/short-haul international

**Wide-Body (Twin-Aisle)**:
- Boeing 777: 300-396 seats
- Airbus A350: 300-410 seats
- Boeing 787 Dreamliner: 242-330 seats
- **Definition**: Two aisles, long-haul international, 8,000-10,000 nm range

### Aircraft Specifications
**MTOW (Maximum Takeoff Weight)**: Maximum certified weight for takeoff including fuel, passengers, cargo
**MLW (Maximum Landing Weight)**: Maximum weight at which aircraft can land
**OEW (Operating Empty Weight)**: Aircraft weight with crew but no fuel/payload
**MZFW (Maximum Zero Fuel Weight)**: Maximum weight without fuel (limited by wing structure)

### Engine Terms
**ETOPS (Extended-range Twin-engine Operational Performance Standards)**: Certification for twin-engine aircraft to fly >60 minutes from diversion airport
- ETOPS-120: 120-minute diversion radius
- ETOPS-180: 180-minute diversion radius
- ETOPS-330: 330-minute diversion radius (newest aircraft like A350, 787)

**APU (Auxiliary Power Unit)**: Gas turbine in tail providing electrical power and bleed air on ground
**Thrust Rating**: Maximum force engines produce, measured in pounds (e.g., "30,000 lb thrust")

## Commercial Terms

### Booking and Inventory
**PNR (Passenger Name Record)**: Unique 6-character code identifying reservation
**Segment**: Single flight leg in journey (e.g., JFK-LHR is one segment)
**O&D (Origin and Destination)**: Passenger's starting point and final destination regardless of connections
**Leg**: Single takeoff-to-landing flight

### Fare Classes
**Booking Codes** (hierarchy highest to lowest):
- **F**: First Class
- **J**: Business Class (long-haul premium)
- **W**: Premium Economy
- **Y**: Full-fare Economy (unrestricted)
- **B/M/H/Q/V**: Discount Economy (various restrictions)
- **N/X/O**: Deep discount/Basic Economy

**RBD (Reservation Booking Designator)**: The specific fare bucket code
**Fare Basis**: Alphanumeric code defining fare rules and restrictions

### Revenue Management Terms
**DSS (Departure Control System)**: System managing check-in, boarding, weight/balance
**PSS (Passenger Service System)**: Comprehensive platform for reservations, inventory, departure control (e.g., Amadeus, Sabre)
**GDS (Global Distribution System)**: Network for travel agents to book flights (Amadeus, Sabre, Travelport)
**Codeshare**: Marketing arrangement where one airline operates flight, another sells tickets under own flight number

## Airport and Ground Operations

### Airport Infrastructure
**Apron/Ramp**: Aircraft parking area at terminal
**Jet Bridge**: Enclosed, movable connector from terminal to aircraft
**Remote Stand**: Aircraft parking position requiring bus transport to terminal
**Deice Pad**: Dedicated area for aircraft de-icing operations

### Ground Handling
**GPU (Ground Power Unit)**: External electrical power for aircraft on ground
**ASU (Air Start Unit)**: External unit providing compressed air for engine start
**Pushback**: Towing aircraft backward away from gate (typically to 90-degree angle)
**Tug/Towbarless Tractor**: Vehicle for pushback operations

### Baggage Terms
**BPM (Baggage Processing Message)**: Electronic message tracking bag through system
**BTMs (Baggage Tags)**: Printed labels with barcode/RFID
**Carousel**: Circular conveyor where arriving baggage delivered
**AHL (Absent Baggage List)**: Record of missing bags
**DPR (Damaged Property Report)**: Documentation of damaged bags

## Flight Operations

### Navigation and Flight Planning
**Flight Plan**: Document filed with ATC containing route, altitude, speed, fuel, alternate airports
**ETE (Estimated Time Enroute)**: Projected flight duration
**ETA (Estimated Time of Arrival)**: Projected arrival time
**ETD (Estimated Time of Departure)**: Projected departure time
**Alternate**: Backup airport if destination becomes unavailable
**Diversion**: Change of destination to alternate airport

### Altitude and Airspace
**FL (Flight Level)**: Altitude in hundreds of feet (FL350 = 35,000 feet)
**Transition Altitude**: Altitude below which use local QNH, above use standard pressure (1013 hPa/29.92 in)
**RVSM (Reduced Vertical Separation Minimum)**: 1,000-foot vertical separation (vs 2,000 feet) above FL290
**Class B/C/D Airspace**: Controlled airspace with specific entry requirements

### Weather Terms
**METAR**: Meteorological Aerodrome Report (current conditions)
**TAF**: Terminal Aerodrome Forecast (forecast for next 9-24 hours)
**SIGMET**: Significant Meteorological Information (hazardous weather)
**NOTAM**: Notice to Air Missions (temporary changes to procedures/facilities)
**CAT (Clear Air Turbulence)**: Turbulence without visible cloud
**Wind Shear**: Sudden change in wind speed/direction

### Communication
**ATC (Air Traffic Control)**: Service managing aircraft traffic
- **Tower**: Controls airport surface and immediate airspace
- **Approach/Departure**: Controls traffic arriving/departing terminal area
- **Center/Enroute**: Controls enroute traffic at cruise altitudes
**Squawk**: Transponder code (4 digits) assigned by ATC
**Mayday**: Distress call (life-threatening emergency)
**Pan-Pan**: Urgent call (urgent but not life-threatening)

## Maintenance Terms

### Inspection Types
**Line Maintenance**: Routine maintenance at gate/hangar (pre-flight, transit, daily)
**Base Maintenance**: Heavy maintenance requiring dedicated facility
**AOG (Aircraft on Ground)**: Aircraft grounded due to technical issue requiring immediate repair
**MEL (Minimum Equipment List)**: Document listing equipment that may be inoperative with specific conditions
**CDL (Configuration Deviation List)**: Parts that may be missing with performance penalties

### Component Tracking
**LRU (Line Replaceable Unit)**: Component replaced at line station (not requiring shop repair)
**TBO (Time Between Overhauls)**: Manufacturer-recommended overhaul interval
**SB (Service Bulletin)**: Manufacturer recommendation for inspection/repair
**AD (Airworthiness Directive)**: Regulatory mandate for safety-critical action

## Crew Terms

### Crew Compositions
**PIC (Pilot in Command)**: Captain, ultimately responsible for flight
**SIC (Second in Command)**: First Officer/Co-pilot
**PF (Pilot Flying)**: Pilot manipulating controls
**PM (Pilot Monitoring)**: Pilot not flying, handling communications/checklists
**Cabin Crew**: Flight attendants/pursers
**Deadhead**: Crew member positioning as passenger for duty
**Jumpseat**: Observer seat in cockpit (authorized personnel only)

### Crew Rest
**Bunk/Bunkie**: Crew rest facility on long-haul aircraft
**Augmented Crew**: Additional pilots allowing extended flight duty periods
**Class 1/2/3 Rest Facility**: Categories of crew rest accommodation
